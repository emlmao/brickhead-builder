Daffodils (WIP)

September 2026 • PLA • 2 hours

💡 Idea

A flower for new beginnings. Daffodils are among the first to bloom after winter, pushing through the cold to stand for rebirth, hope, and the courage it takes to start again. Inspired by a friend in Seattle who loves daffodils, this one is made to keep that brightness going through the gray months and long after the season has passed.

About

Hi, I'm Emily! I've always liked making things. From a kid making perler beads, lanyard keychains, friendship bracelets, clay figures, and rainbow loom bracelets, to an adult building Legos, coding projects, and now 3D printing — I want to create objects that are beautiful and meaningful.
